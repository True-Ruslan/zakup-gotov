# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Zakup Gotov aims to be a technically rigorous and respectful open collaboration space.

Expected behavior includes:

- discussing ideas and code rather than attacking people;
- giving specific, evidence-based feedback;
- acknowledging uncertainty and correcting mistakes;
- respecting different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints;
- protecting private, security-sensitive, and personal information;
- keeping issues and reviews relevant to the project.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption, publishing private/security-sensitive information, or repeated bad-faith interaction.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributions that violate this policy and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and the project.

For conduct concerns that should not be public, contact the repository owner privately through an appropriate verified channel.

Maintainers will try to evaluate reports fairly, proportionately, and with appropriate confidentiality.
